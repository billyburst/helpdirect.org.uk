Blackjack: The UK's Favourite Table Game

Blackjack is consistently the most popular table game at UK online casinos, and new sites offer an impressive range of variants to keep things interesting. The core objective remains the same across all versions — get as close to 21 as possible without going over, and beat the dealer's hand — but the rules, side bets, and payouts can vary significantly between different versions.

Common blackjack variants you will find at new UK casinos include:

  • Classic Blackjack: The standard version played with one to eight decks, featuring the traditional rules most players are familiar with.
  • European Blackjack: The dealer receives only one card initially (no hole card), which slightly changes the strategy compared to the American version.
  • Blackjack Switch: You play two hands simultaneously and have the option to switch the top cards between them, creating interesting strategic decisions.
  • Perfect Pairs Blackjack: Includes a side bet that pays out if your first two cards form a pair (mixed, coloured, or perfect).
  • Infinite Blackjack: A live dealer variant by Evolution where unlimited players can join the same table, making it easier to find a seat and often offering lower minimum bets.
  • Speed Blackjack: A faster-paced live dealer version where the player who makes their decision first gets served first, rewarding quick thinking.

One of the key advantages of blackjack for strategic players is its low house edge. When played with perfect basic strategy, standard blackjack has a house edge of around 0.5%, making it one of the most player-friendly games in any casino.

Roulette: Classic Elegance in Multiple Formats

Roulette is the second most popular table game at UK casinos, and new sites offer a rich variety of versions. The three main variants remain European, French, and American, but modern online casinos have expanded far beyond these classics.

  • European Roulette: Features a single zero on the wheel, giving a house edge of 2.7%. This is the standard version most UK players prefer.
  • French Roulette: Similar to European but includes the La Partage rule, which returns half your even-money bet if the ball lands on zero. This reduces the house edge on even-money bets to just 1.35%.
  • American Roulette: Features both a single zero and a double zero, increasing the house edge to 5.26%. Generally less favourable for players but still widely available.
  • Lightning Roulette: An Evolution live dealer game that adds random multipliers of up to 500x to specific numbers on every round, creating the potential for much larger payouts on straight-up bets.
  • Immersive Roulette: A live dealer version with multiple camera angles and slow-motion replays of the ball landing, creating a cinematic viewing experience.
  • Auto Roulette: A live game that uses an automated wheel with no human dealer, allowing for faster round times and often available around the clock.

When choosing a roulette variant, always opt for European or French roulette over American roulette. The single zero gives you significantly better odds over the long term.

Baccarat: Simple Yet Sophisticated

Baccarat has seen a huge surge in popularity at UK online casinos in recent years, partly driven by its prominence in live dealer lobbies. The game is beautifully simple — you bet on whether the Player hand, the Banker hand, or a Tie will win. Cards are dealt according to fixed rules, meaning there are no decisions for you to make beyond placing your bet.

Key things to know about baccarat at new UK casinos:

  • Banker bet is the best option: The Banker bet has a house edge of approximately 1.06%, making it one of the best bets in any casino game. A 5% commission is typically charged on winning Banker bets.
  • Avoid the Tie bet: While it pays 8:1 or 9:1 at most casinos, the Tie bet carries a house edge of over 14%, making it a poor value wager.
  • Speed Baccarat: A faster version available in live dealer format where results are revealed more quickly, typically with a 25-second betting window.
  • Baccarat Squeeze: A live dealer variant where the dealer slowly peels back the cards to reveal their values, adding drama and tension to each hand.

Poker: Multiple Variants for Every Skill Level

Online poker at UK casinos comes in several different forms, and it is important to understand the distinction between them. Casino poker games are played against the house, not against other players, making them quite different from tournament or cash game poker.

The most common casino poker variants at new UK sites include:

  • Casino Hold'em: Based on Texas Hold'em, you play against the dealer. You receive two hole cards and share five community cards. You must decide whether to call or fold after seeing the flop.
  • Three Card Poker: A fast-paced game where you and the dealer each receive three cards. You decide whether your hand is strong enough to play or whether to fold. Side bets like Pair Plus add extra excitement.
  • Caribbean Stud Poker: A five-card poker game played against the dealer. The key strategic decision is whether to raise or fold based on your five cards and the one dealer card you can see.
  • Ultimate Texas Hold'em: A more complex variant where you can raise at multiple points during the hand, with the potential for larger payouts if you time your raises correctly.

Live Dealer vs RNG Table Games

At new UK casinos, you will typically find table games in two formats: RNG (Random Number Generator) versions and live dealer versions. Understanding the differences helps you choose the right format for your preferences.

RNG table games are fully digital. The outcomes are determined by software algorithms, and you play at your own pace against a computer. The advantages of RNG games include:

  • Available 24/7 with no waiting for a seat
  • Lower minimum bets, often starting from as little as 10p or 20p
  • Faster pace — you can play more hands per hour
  • Demo versions available so you can practise for free

Live dealer table games are streamed in real time from professional studio environments. A human dealer manages the game, and you interact via a chat interface. The advantages of live dealer games include:

  • More immersive and authentic casino atmosphere
  • Social interaction with the dealer and sometimes other players
  • Greater trust for players who prefer seeing a real person handle the cards and wheel
  • Unique game variants not available in RNG format, such as Lightning Roulette and Crazy Time
