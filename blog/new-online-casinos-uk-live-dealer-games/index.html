What Are Live Dealer Games?

Live dealer games are casino games streamed in real time from professional studios or actual casino floors. A human dealer manages the game while players participate through their browser or mobile device, placing bets digitally while watching the action unfold via high-definition video feed. This format combines the social interaction and transparency of a land-based casino with the convenience of playing from home.

Unlike standard online casino games that use random number generators (RNG) to determine outcomes, live dealer games use physical cards, roulette wheels, and dice. Players can see every card dealt and every spin of the wheel, which provides a level of trust and engagement that software-based games cannot replicate. Most live games also feature a chat function, allowing you to interact with the dealer and sometimes with other players at the table.

Types of Live Dealer Games Available

New UK casinos typically offer a comprehensive selection of live dealer games. Here are the main categories you can expect to find:

Live Blackjack

Live blackjack remains the most popular table game at UK casinos. Standard versions accommodate seven players per table, with unlimited blackjack variants allowing any number of players to participate simultaneously. Betting limits typically range from £1 to £10,000, though VIP tables can go much higher. Many new casinos offer multiple blackjack variants including Speed Blackjack, which reduces waiting time between hands, and Lightning Blackjack, which adds random multipliers to winning hands.

Live Roulette

Live roulette is available in European, French, and American formats, though European roulette with its single zero is the most common at UK sites. Lightning Roulette, which applies random multipliers of up to 500x to straight-up number bets, has become enormously popular. Other variants include Speed Roulette, Auto Roulette with automated wheel spins, and Immersive Roulette with cinematic camera angles that follow the ball in slow motion.

Live Baccarat

Baccarat is particularly popular with high-stakes players. Live versions include standard baccarat, Speed Baccarat, and Lightning Baccarat with multiplied payouts. Most new casinos offer multiple baccarat tables with varying betting limits to accommodate different bankroll sizes.

Live Poker

Casino poker variants dominate the live poker category. Three Card Poker, Casino Hold'em, Caribbean Stud, and Ultimate Texas Hold'em are all commonly available. These games pit the player against the house rather than other players, making them accessible to all skill levels. Some new casinos also offer live poker tournament formats through specialised software.

Live Game Shows

This is where the live casino space has seen the most innovation. Game show titles blend entertainment with gambling, creating interactive experiences unlike traditional table games. The most popular include:

  • Crazy Time: A money wheel game with four bonus rounds, offering massive multiplier potential and a highly entertaining presentation.
  • Deal or No Deal Live: Based on the television show, players open briefcases and decide whether to accept the banker's offer.
  • Monopoly Live: Combines a money wheel with an augmented reality Monopoly board for bonus rounds.
  • Lightning Dice: A simple dice game enhanced with random multipliers on specific totals.
  • Funky Time: Evolution's latest large-scale game show with multiple bonus features and a disco theme.

What to Look for in a Live Casino at a New UK Site

Not all live casino offerings are equal. When evaluating a new casino's live dealer section, consider the following factors:

  • Provider range: Casinos partnering with multiple live providers offer greater variety. A site with both Evolution and Pragmatic Play, for example, will have a much broader game selection than one relying on a single provider.
  • Streaming quality: Live games require stable, high-definition video streams. Test the games on your device and internet connection before committing significant funds. Buffering, lag, or low-resolution streams can ruin the experience and potentially affect gameplay.
  • Betting limits: Ensure the casino offers tables within your preferred betting range. Casual players need tables starting at £0.50 to £1, while high rollers should look for VIP tables with limits of £5,000 or more per hand.
  • Table availability: Popular tables can fill up during peak hours, particularly standard blackjack tables. Casinos with more table capacity and unlimited-player formats reduce the chance of having to wait for a seat.
  • Mobile compatibility: Live dealer games are demanding on mobile devices. The best implementations offer the same quality and functionality on smartphones and tablets as on desktop, with touch-optimised interfaces and efficient bandwidth usage.
  • Dedicated and exclusive tables: Some new casinos negotiate exclusive branded tables with providers, offering a more personalised environment with dedicated dealers and custom studio designs.

Live Casino on Mobile

Playing live dealer games on mobile has become seamless at the best new UK casinos. Modern live casino software automatically adjusts to screen size, repositioning betting interfaces and game information for touch interaction.
