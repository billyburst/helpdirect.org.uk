East Lancashire Safe Trader

Safe Trader in East Lancashire

The Safe Trader scheme in East Lancashire provides residents across Burnley, Blackburn with Darwen, Hyndburn, Pendle, Ribble Valley, and Rossendale with access to thoroughly vetted and approved local traders. This trading standards approved scheme ensures you can hire tradespeople with confidence.

Why Use East Lancashire Safe Trader?

Finding reliable traders can be challenging, but the Safe Trader scheme makes it easier by pre-vetting businesses on your behalf. Every trader in the scheme has been checked and approved by Lancashire County Council Trading Standards.

Key Benefits

  • All traders vetted by Trading Standards
  • Verified insurance and qualifications
  • Regular monitoring and customer feedback reviews
  • Support with complaint resolution
  • No call-out fees charged by most traders

Areas Covered

The East Lancashire Safe Trader scheme covers the following areas:

  • Burnley - Including Padiham, Hapton, and surrounding villages
  • Blackburn with Darwen - All areas within the borough
  • Hyndburn - Including Accrington, Great Harwood, Clayton-le-Moors, and Oswaldtwistle
  • Pendle - Including Nelson, Colne, Barnoldswick, and Brierfield
  • Ribble Valley - Including Clitheroe, Longridge, and Whalley
  • Rossendale - Including Rawtenstall, Bacup, Haslingden, and Ramsbottom

How to Find an East Lancashire Safe Trader

Finding a Safe Trader approved business in East Lancashire is straightforward:

  1. Visit the Lancashire County Council Trading Standards website
  2. Search the Safe Trader directory by trade type or location
  3. Review trader profiles, qualifications, and customer feedback
  4. Contact multiple traders for quotes and comparisons
  5. Choose the trader that best meets your needs

What Checks Are Performed?

Before a business can join the East Lancashire Safe Trader scheme, they must pass comprehensive checks including:

  • Business registration and credentials verification
  • Public liability insurance confirmation (minimum £2 million)
  • Professional indemnity insurance where applicable
  • Trading standards history review
  • References from previous customers
  • Relevant trade qualifications and certifications

Consumer Advice

While Safe Trader approved businesses have been vetted, you should still follow best practices when hiring any trader:

  • Always get written quotes before work begins
  • Ensure the scope of work is clearly defined
  • Agree payment terms in advance (never pay in full upfront)
  • Keep records of all communications and agreements
  • Check that appropriate insurance is in place
  • Ask to see examples of previous work

Support and Complaints

The Safe Trader scheme provides ongoing support to both consumers and traders. If you experience problems with a Safe Trader approved business, Lancashire County Council Trading Standards can offer advice and assistance with resolving disputes.

All complaints are taken seriously and investigated thoroughly. Businesses that fail to maintain standards or receive serious complaints may be removed from the scheme.
