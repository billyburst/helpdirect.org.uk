Safe Trader in Lancaster, Chorley & West Lancashire

The Safe Trader scheme serves residents across Lancaster, Chorley, and West Lancashire, providing access to thoroughly vetted and approved local tradespeople. Whether you live in Lancaster city, Morecambe, Chorley town, Ormskirk, Skelmersdale, or the surrounding villages, you can find trustworthy traders through this trading standards approved scheme.

Why Choose Safe Trader?

Finding reliable traders can be difficult, especially in rural areas where word-of-mouth recommendations may be limited. The Safe Trader scheme solves this problem by providing a directory of pre-vetted businesses that have been thoroughly checked by Lancashire County Council Trading Standards.

Peace of Mind Benefits

  • All traders approved by Lancashire Trading Standards
  • Comprehensive insurance verification
  • Regular performance monitoring
  • Customer review system
  • Support with dispute resolution
  • Protection against rogue traders

How to Use the Safe Trader Scheme

Finding and hiring a Safe Trader approved business is straightforward:

  1. Access the directory: Visit the Lancashire County Council Trading Standards website
  2. Search for traders: Filter by trade type, location, or business name
  3. Review profiles: Check qualifications, specialisms, and customer reviews
  4. Get multiple quotes: Contact several traders to compare prices and availability
  5. Check credentials: Verify insurance documents and relevant certifications
  6. Agree terms: Ensure you have a written quote and clear agreement
  7. Leave feedback: After work is complete, share your experience

Trader Vetting Process

Before joining the Safe Trader scheme, businesses undergo rigorous checks including:

Mandatory Checks

  • Business registration and company information
  • Public liability insurance (minimum £2 million cover)
  • Employers' liability insurance where applicable
  • Professional indemnity insurance for certain trades
  • Trading standards compliance history
  • Relevant trade qualifications and memberships
  • Gas Safe, NICEIC, or other required registrations
  • Customer references

Consumer Tips and Advice

While Safe Trader businesses have been vetted, it's still important to follow best practices:

Before Hiring

  • Get at least three written quotes for comparison
  • Ensure quotes include VAT and all costs
  • Check the trader's insurance is current
  • Ask to see examples of previous work
  • Request references from recent customers
  • Verify trade qualifications are genuine

During the Work

  • Ensure you have a written contract or agreement
  • Never pay the full amount upfront
  • Agree a payment schedule tied to work completion
  • Keep records of all payments and communications
  • Raise concerns promptly if they arise
  • Inspect work at each stage before payment

After Completion

  • Ensure all work is completed to satisfaction
  • Get guarantees and warranties in writing
  • Keep all paperwork and receipts
  • Leave honest feedback on the Safe Trader directory
  • Report any issues to the trader first, then Trading Standards if unresolved

Support and Complaints

Lancashire County Council Trading Standards provides ongoing support for the Safe Trader scheme. If you experience problems with a Safe Trader business:

  1. Contact the trader first: Many issues can be resolved through direct communication
  2. Document everything: Keep records of all correspondence and evidence
  3. Contact Trading Standards: They can offer advice and mediation support
  4. Formal complaint: Submit a complaint through the proper channels if needed

Trading Standards takes all complaints seriously and will investigate issues thoroughly. Businesses that fail to maintain standards may be removed from the scheme.

Benefits for Rural Areas

The Safe Trader scheme is particularly valuable for residents in rural parts of Lancaster District, Chorley, and West Lancashire where:

  • Traditional word-of-mouth recommendations may be limited
  • Traveling traders may be harder to verify
  • Distance can make checking credentials difficult
  • Limited internet access makes online research challenging

Safe Trader provides assurance that businesses have been properly vetted regardless of where you live in the region.

Special Considerations

For Vulnerable Residents

Safe Trader is especially helpful for elderly or vulnerable residents who may be targeted by rogue traders. The pre-vetting process and ongoing monitoring provide additional protection.

Carers, family members, and support workers can use the Safe Trader directory to find reputable traders on behalf of those they support.

For Holiday Homes and Rental Properties

Landlords and holiday home owners in Lancaster, Chorley, and West Lancashire can use Safe Trader to find reliable contractors for property maintenance, even when they're not local to the area.

Emergency and Urgent Work

While Safe Trader is ideal for planned work, the directory can also help you find traders for emergency repairs. Many Safe Trader businesses offer emergency call-out services for urgent plumbing, heating, electrical, and roofing issues.
